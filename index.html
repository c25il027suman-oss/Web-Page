Immense Yourself: Things to Do

Experience the authentic lifestyle, rich cultural heritage, and distinct flavors unique to the hills of East Nepal.

सांस्कृतिक अनुभव

Kirat Heritage & Chandi Dance

Witness the vibrant soul of the local Rai and Limbu communities. If you visit during the Udhauli or Ubhauli festivals, you will experience the mesmerizing rhythmic steps of the traditional Chandi Naach, celebrating nature and ancestral heritage.

✨
Insider Local Tip

When entering a traditional home, you might be greeted with a hand-woven Dhaka topi or a khada scarf as a mark of high respect. It's a warm, unforgettable introduction to our hospitality.

स्थानीय परिकार

Authentic Eastern Cuisine

Savor the distinctive tastes of the eastern hills. Beyond the staple organic Dal Bhat, treat your palate to local delicacies like Kinema (fermented soybeans with a rich, pungent aroma) and fiery wild-pepper chutneys made from fresh mountain herbs.

Spiced Sekuwa & Sukuti

Nothing pairs better with evening mountain mist than open-fire grilled meat (Sekuwa) or traditional air-dried jerky cooked with local aromatic ginger, garlic, and wild spices.
